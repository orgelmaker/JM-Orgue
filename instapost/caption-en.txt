JM-Orgue + JM-Rec are both out, and they work together.

JM-Orgue is a virtual pipe organ for Windows, macOS and Linux: hook up a
MIDI organ, pick a sampleset and play. Reads GrandOrgue and Hauptwerk
samplesets, with stops, couplers, swell boxes, tremulant, reverb and a
general crescendo. Seven languages, no dongle, no subscription — and
you may share it with friends.

JM-Rec is the recording wizard to sample an organ yourself. Pipe by pipe,
keyboard by keyboard. Put a computer on the gallery, walk to the console
with your phone, scan a QR code and control JM-Rec from the organ bench
— record, pause, stop, next stop — no app to install. The result is a
folder in the format JM-Orgue reads straight away. No converting, no
renaming: you open the set in JM-Orgue and play.

Two programs. One workflow. Both free.
