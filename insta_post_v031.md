## Caption (EN — voor internationale post)

🌍 JM-Orgue update!

The app now speaks **four languages**: Dutch, English, French, German. Switch instantly via Appearance & Layout. ~200 translation keys across the whole app.

🎙️ Our mission stays the same: **JM-Rec** — the companion tool that lets you sample your own church organ. Connect a microphone and a MIDI keyboard, JM-Rec walks through every pipe, every stop, every coupler. It writes samples in the correct folder structure, with loop points + release tails + a ready-to-use `.organ` definition. Open it in **JM-Orgue** afterwards and play your own organ at home — no technical knowledge required.

🧩 New this month:
▸ Multilingual UI (NL/EN/FR/DE) throughout the app
▸ Settings split — App Settings (audio, MIDI, appearance, language) separated from Organ Settings (reverb, EQ, voicing per organ)
▸ 3 tabs: Organ · Organ Settings · App Settings (F1/F2/F3)
▸ Wind groups — 1 to 8 wind supplies configurable
▸ Universal MIDI learn — right-click on desktop, 3-sec long-press on touch

🧪 **Looking for testers for JM-Rec!** Are you an organist who wants to sample your own church organ? Send me a **direct message**.

⏳ **JM-Rec and JM-Orgue are not yet publicly available.** Release is expected **during 2026**. The current beta is closed — by invitation only after personal contact.

What you need to test: access to an organ, a Windows PC with microphone and MIDI keyboard, and willingness to give feedback.
